# **aiarbitration.us**  
### **U.S.-Law‑Bound Arbitration Forum for AI, Telecom, and Digital Systems**

`aiarbitration.us` is a forthcoming arbitration venue designed for the AI era—where disputes involving automated systems, operators, telecom infrastructure, and digital contracts require **machine‑verifiable evidence**, **deterministic procedures**, and **U.S.‑anchored legal clarity**.

This repository contains the static placeholder site and foundational architecture for the forum.

---

## **Purpose**

Modern AI systems generate logs, signatures, and machine‑readable artifacts that traditional arbitration venues are not equipped to handle.  
**aiarbitration.us** provides:

- A **neutral, U.S.-jurisdictional anchor** for AI‑related disputes  
- A venue where **operators, counterparties, and automated systems** can rely on predictable rules  
- A framework for **evidence intake**, **log verification**, and **model‑generated reasoning artifacts**  
- Infrastructure aligned with **regulators, telecom standards, and sovereign operator practices**

---

## **Technical Direction**

The forum is being designed around:

### **✔ Clinfera + ONNX Legal Reasoning Models**  
Used for structured intake, evidence classification, and procedural routing.

### **✔ Operator‑Grade, Deterministic Infrastructure**  
Built to support sovereign workflows, auditability, and regulator‑aligned behavior.

### **✔ Machine‑Readable Evidence Channels**  
Including logs, signatures, call detail records, and model‑generated reasoning traces.

### **✔ Static, Zero‑Tracking Public Surface**  
The initial site is a static HTML/SVG placeholder with no cookies, analytics, or client‑side execution.
