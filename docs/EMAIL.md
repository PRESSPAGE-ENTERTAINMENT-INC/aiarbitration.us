# Recommended Emails
**For an AI‑driven arbitration forum like aiarbitration.us, you should establish a small, deterministic set of role‑based emails that mirror court‑grade operational structure.** Clerk@ is the correct anchor, and you build outward from there.

Below is a structured, comprehensive set of recommended addresses — grouped by operational function — that fit your PRESSPAGE/Clinfera architecture, chain‑of‑custody requirements, and your preference for deterministic, audit‑ready workflows.

---

## 🏛️ Core Arbitration Operations (must‑have)
These are the backbone of a neutral forum. They map cleanly to your clerk workstation, chain‑of‑custody, and AI arbitrator workflow.

- **clerk@aiarbitration.us** — *Primary human clerk inbox; certification, notices, scheduling.*
- **intake@aiarbitration.us** — *Receives initial filings, respondent submissions, attachments.*
- **notices@aiarbitration.us** — *Automated service of notices, deadlines, continuances, and procedural updates.*
- **awards@aiarbitration.us** — *Outbound certified awards, final determinations, and PDF packets.*
- **records@aiarbitration.us** — *Chain‑of‑custody logs, evidence receipts, timestamped submissions.*
- **support@aiarbitration.us** — *General help for parties who are not part of the case record.*

These map directly to your deterministic clerk UI screens and the chain‑of‑custody model you’ve already built.

---

## 🤖 AI Arbitrator & System Automation
These addresses separate human‑facing communication from machine‑generated artifacts.

- **arbitrator@aiarbitration.us** — *AI arbitrator output channel (non‑interactive).*
- **factfinding@aiarbitration.us** — *Outputs from /factfinding route.*
- **scan@aiarbitration.us** — *Outputs from /scan route (rulebook checks, procedural flags).*
- **noreply@aiarbitration.us** — *System‑generated messages that must not accept replies.*

This keeps your AI arbitrator positioned as an expert‑system engine, not a human decision‑maker.

---

## 🧾 Compliance, Legal, and Regulatory
These support your SEC/CFTC alignment, procedural fairness, and audit‑grade recordkeeping.

- **compliance@aiarbitration.us** — *Internal compliance, audit trails, rulebook updates.*
- **legal@aiarbitration.us** — *Formal correspondence with counsel, regulators, or corporate entities.*
- **subpoena@aiarbitration.us** — *For certified record requests or lawful demands.*
- **privacy@aiarbitration.us** — *Data handling, confidentiality, and PII‑related inquiries.*

This mirrors how FINRA, AAA, and JAMS structure their inbound channels.

---

## 🧩 Developer, Integration, and System Health
These support your deterministic, offline‑first, chain‑of‑custody architecture.

- **dev@aiarbitration.us** — *Developer communications, API issues, integration testing.*
- **system@aiarbitration.us** — *Automated health checks, uptime alerts, internal logs.*
- **security@aiarbitration.us** — *Security reports, authentication issues, breach notifications.*

Given your zero‑password, push‑only authentication model, this is essential.

---

## 🧑‍⚖️ Party‑Facing Convenience Addresses
These help respondents and claimants navigate the system without exposing clerk operations.

- **respondent@aiarbitration.us** — *Respondent login issues, access to the agreement, first‑time onboarding.*
- **claimant@aiarbitration.us** — *Claimant filing questions, fee waivers, procedural guidance.*
- **service@aiarbitration.us** — *Proof‑of‑service confirmations, delivery receipts.*

These reduce noise hitting clerk@ and keep your workflow deterministic.

---

## ⭐ Optional but Highly Useful

- **media@aiarbitration.us** — *Press inquiries (especially relevant once you integrate PRESSPAGE).*
