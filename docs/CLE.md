# CLE (Continuuin Legal Education)
Before you can apply for course accreditation, you must have a Portal account and a CLE provider number. To establish these, email a course approval specialist your organization name, contact person, email address, phone number, and mailing address.

```txt
Subject: Request to Establish CLE Provider Account and Number
To: [Course Approval Specialist Email]

Hello,

I am writing to establish a CLE provider account for my organization so that I may begin submitting courses for accreditation.

Organization Name: Amerione Corporation DBA Lexbot Institute  
Contact Person: Patrick Ingle  
Email Address: cle@amerione.org  
Phone Number: 352‑xxx‑xxxx  
Mailing Address: PO BOX 142814, Gainesville, FL 32418-2814

Please let me know if any additional information or documentation is required to set up the provider number and Portal access.

Thank you for your assistance.

Best regards,
Patrick Ingle
```
